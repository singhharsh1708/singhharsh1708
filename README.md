### SciML, 125 merged PRs

- [OrdinaryDiffEq.jl](https://github.com/SciML/OrdinaryDiffEq.jl) - the ODE time integrators, and where most of my work lives (104 merged). I added the serial spectral deferred correction solver family, the explicit MRI-GARK multirate methods, the order-10 MSRK10 tableau, and a long tail of correctness fixes across the Rosenbrock, FIRK and ESDIRK families.
- [SciMLBenchmarks.jl](https://github.com/SciML/SciMLBenchmarks.jl) - work-precision benchmarks for the multirate and stiff solver families.
- [ModelingToolkit.jl](https://github.com/SciML/ModelingToolkit.jl) - symbolic-numeric modelling.
- [SciMLOperators.jl](https://github.com/SciML/SciMLOperators.jl) - lazy operator algebra.
- [NonlinearSolve.jl](https://github.com/SciML/NonlinearSolve.jl) and [SciMLBase.jl](https://github.com/SciML/SciMLBase.jl) - the solver interfaces underneath all of it.
